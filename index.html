5-MINUTE MICRO LESSONS
01The golden rule,
campus edition+KINDNESS · 5 MIN READ

“Do not do to others what you do not want done to yourself.” This idea, associated with Analects 15.24, starts with a simple pause: how would this feel from the other side?

Before sharing an embarrassing photo, ask for permission. Before dismissing a classmate’s question, remember your first day. Reciprocity turns an abstract principle into a daily habit.

Try it today: Think of one small boundary you value. Offer someone else the same consideration.
02Different minds.
Different ways to grow.+WISDOM · 5 MIN READ

In the Analects, Confucius sometimes gives different advice to different students. Learning is personal: a cautious friend might need encouragement, while an impulsive friend may benefit from reflection.

On campus, this can mean adjusting how you study, mentor, or collaborate. Treating people with equal care does not require treating everyone identically.

Try it today: Ask a teammate what kind of support helps them learn best.
03Disagree, without
disconnecting.+HARMONY · 5 MIN READ

Harmony is not the same as agreement. Analects 13.23 contrasts harmony with mere sameness: people can remain connected while holding different views.

In a debate, name what you have understood before explaining where you differ. Respect does not demand silence about unfairness; thoughtful disagreement can strengthen a group.

Try it today: Begin a difficult conversation with “I see why that matters to you.”
